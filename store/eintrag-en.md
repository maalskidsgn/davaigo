## Beschreibung (max. 4000)
```
Russian looks foreign. Two weeks from now, you'll be reading Cyrillic.

Davaigo takes you from your first letter to the edge of B2, in 180 lessons
built on one another. Short enough for a coffee break, in an order that makes
sense.

THE CYRILLIC ALPHABET, LEARNED PROPERLY
The first 18 lessons are all about reading. Letter by letter, always with real
words. The six false friends (В Н Р С У Х) get their own place, so you won't
misread them for a year.

HEAR EVERY WORD
Every word, example sentence and line of dialogue comes with audio, more than
4,000 recordings. It matters more than it sounds: Russian stress moves around,
and an о only sounds like "o" when it's stressed. молоко is pronounced
"malakó". You learn that by listening.

FLASHCARDS THAT THINK AHEAD
Words you know come back less often. Words you hesitate on come back sooner.
The gap grows with every right answer.

LEARN FROM REAL VIDEOS
Watch Russian YouTube videos with subtitles that follow along. Tap any word to
see its meaning and save it to your trainer. Every minute you watch earns XP.

STAY WITH IT
Daily streaks, milestones, a leaderboard, friends and duels. Plus games for the
days when you don't feel like studying: memory, word search, hangman and more.

YOUR PROGRESS GOES WITH YOU
Sign in and your words and progress are there on every device.
```
