2. What Davaigo processes from you

Without an account

You can view the homepage and go through the introductory questionnaire without creating an account. We do not store any personal data on our servers. Your answers and the chosen starter package are stored only on your device until you sign up.

With an account

For an account, we need your email address and a password; optionally, you can provide a display name. Additionally, while learning, the following are generated: your collected vocabulary with learning progress, completed lessons, points, levels and daily streak, saved videos and songs, and – with Premium – generated e-books.

The legal basis is Art. 6 para. 1 lit. b GDPR: Without this data, the service cannot be provided, as this is its essence.

Server logs

Technical log data (IP address, time, accessed address, browser type) is generated upon access. This data is used for secure operation and is deleted shortly thereafter. The legal basis is Art. 6 para. 1 lit. f GDPR – our legitimate interest in a functioning, secure service.

What the speaking trainer stores

We do not store your voice. We store the conversation as text, a summary, detected mistakes and new words, and a learning profile with your goals, interests, estimated level and progress. This lets the trainer refer to earlier calls and lets you read them again. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time by deleting individual conversations or the whole memory on the trainer page and no longer using the trainer. This data is also deleted with your account. We keep the start and duration of each call to calculate your minutes. The analysis is automated and may contain errors. It has no legal effect on you.

4. Cookies and storage on your device

Davaigo stores your learning progress in your browser's local storage so the app works offline and starts quickly. This is technically necessary (§ 25 para. 2 no. 2 TDDDG) and does not require consent. Supabase sets a login cookie as long as you are logged in.

Cookies for reach measurement (Google Analytics) are set only with your consent via the notice on your first visit. Until you click there, no Google script is loaded and no cookie is set. You can change your decision at any time under 'More → Measurement'; upon withdrawal, the cookies are deleted.

No advertising networks, tracking pixels, or resale of your data take place.

5. How long we store

We store account data and learning progress as long as your account exists. If you delete your account under 'More → Delete account', all associated data will be irretrievably removed. We retain billing data for ten years due to tax obligations (§ 147 AO).

6. Your rights

You have the right to access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20), and objection (Art. 21 GDPR). You can withdraw any consent given at any time with future effect.

For access and erasure, simply send an email to lernen@davaigo.de. You can also download your entire learning progress at any time as a file under 'More → Create backup'.

You can lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. The competent authority for us is the State Commissioner for Data Protection Lower Saxony, Prinzenstraße 5, 30159 Hannover.

7. Changes
